Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Expressive Design LLC collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your project or inquiry.

We may also receive limited technical information through the service providers that help operate this website, such as information necessary to load website resources, process forms, and maintain website security and functionality.

We use information you submit to respond to inquiries, discuss potential design projects, schedule appointments, communicate with you about requested services, and operate and improve our website and business.

We do not sell personal information submitted through our website.

3. Contact Form Processing

Our website uses Formspree to process contact form submissions. Information entered into the contact form is transmitted through Formspree so that we can receive and respond to your inquiry. Formspree may process information according to its own privacy practices and terms.

4. Third-Party Website Services

This website uses third-party resources to provide fonts, icons, styling, and other website functionality. These services may receive technical information from your browser when their resources are loaded.

Our website currently uses resources provided by Google Fonts / Google Material Symbols and Tailwind CSS.

If we add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy and any related consent tools as appropriate.

We may share information with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process inquiries, communicate with you, or provide requested services. 7. Data Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have submitted through this website or to request that we update or delete information when reasonably possible and subject to any legal or business recordkeeping requirements.
