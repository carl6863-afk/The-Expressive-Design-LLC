Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Expressive Design LLC is committed to providing a website that is usable by as many people as reasonably possible.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Our Commitment

We aim to improve the accessibility and usability of this website and to consider accessibility in our ongoing website updates.

Accessibility Features

We work to use clear page structure, readable text, descriptive links, alternative text for meaningful images where appropriate, keyboard-friendly navigation, and sufficient visual contrast.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As technology, content, and recognized accessibility practices evolve, we may make additional changes to improve the website experience.

Third-Party Content

Some parts of the website may rely on third-party services, links, or content that we do not fully control. We encourage third-party providers to offer accessible experiences, but we cannot guarantee the accessibility of external websites or services.

Need Assistance?

If you have difficulty accessing information on this website, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need information in another format, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to help provide the information or assistance you need.
